# Finding Plankton
Plankton trading cards associated with Finding Plankton: A marine science scavenger hunt for local communities.
Budroe, H.M. and Bik, H.M. (2026), Finding Plankton: A Marine Science Scavenger Hunt for Local Communities. Limnology and Oceanography Bulletin, 35: 87-92. https://doi.org/10.1002/lob.70041

Cards are uploaded as .svg format. Reach out to hbudroe@uga.edu with questions or for other formats.
